# Editorial and evidence boundaries

## Reference method

The site adapts anonymized fieldwork observations, specialist enterprise Skill methods and market-advisor orchestration concepts. It preserves the useful working pattern:

Business question → multiple professional perspectives → explicit evidence and unknowns → usable output → real-task evaluation → reusable capability.

The site does not reproduce raw source documents, private datasets, personal profiles, client identifiers, internal organization names, system addresses or credentials.

## Marketing position

Lead with valuable business work: market entry, customer development, relevant outreach, marketing production, frontline opportunity capture, business analysis, planning, intelligence and knowledge reuse.

Approvals and permission controls are implementation boundaries, not the central value proposition. FDE differentiation is in reconstructing real work, making judgment explicit, engineering a complete workflow and retaining useful corrections.

The service architecture is a proposed modular packaging model. It is not a claim that every capability is already a standardized, commercially available product. Availability, dependencies and scope must be confirmed per engagement.

## Claim levels

Each play states its basis:

- Project outputs, SOPs, data modules or workflow materials support some methods.
- Market-entry methods are supported by orchestration and report structures.
- Inventory/production planning is a proposed specialist Skill adaptation.
- Content production depends on tooling, assets and runtime.
- Packaging, compilation or generation alone do not establish business effectiveness.

The 66 additional applications live inside the More tab of their corresponding core play. Each includes a business question, inputs, method and representative outputs. Basis labels distinguish project-derived methods, application prototypes and enterprise Skill extensions. These labels describe the reference material; they do not claim that every application is a production deployment, finished product, independently delivered project or completed customer case. Several applications can derive from different tasks in one project. Scope and integration dependencies are confirmed per engagement.

Source material is translated into anonymized business patterns, not reproduced. Public copy omits project and client identities, internal paths, private data, scoring formulas and deployment details. Imported open-source projects and catalog Skills are not presented as proprietary inventions. The added clinic application concerns service coordination and approved education, not diagnosis or prescribing.

No verified customer ROI, success percentage, customer count, guaranteed timeline or pricing is advertised.

## Samples and attribution

Every sample is synthetic. Sample tables are illustrations, not anonymized real account records. Professional perspectives do not imply participation, authorization or endorsement by any real individual. AI inference must never be presented as an expert quote.

Production engagements should distinguish original evidence, business claims, public statements, third-party assessments and inference. Public collection does not establish ownership or license to redistribute an external Skill.

## Scope of the website

This website explains services and methods. It does not run Agents, gather business inputs, send outreach, access customer systems or schedule consultations.

The pilot brief is a downloadable Markdown template. The visitor completes it locally and shares it only through a channel they choose. Basic hosting access logs are distinct from business-data collection.
